Workshop Disclosures & Policies

CEO Mode is designed to inspire aspiring entrepreneurs to recognize what is already possible with their knowledge, skills, and everyday tools. This document explains the workshop's educational purpose, participant responsibility, and important limitations.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

CEO Mode · Official Workshop Policy
Plain-language notice: This document provides general information, not legal, tax, accounting, investment, or financial advice. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional who understands your situation and location.

1. Educational and Inspirational Purpose

CEO Mode is an educational workshop created to inspire excitement, hope, and purposeful action around entrepreneurship. It provides examples, frameworks, prompts, and practical ways to think about turning existing skills into a possible business direction. The workshop does not form a professional advisory, employment, partnership, agency, or fiduciary relationship.

2. No Guaranteed Income or Business Results

No specific income, profit, customer, follower, engagement, launch, or business result is promised or guaranteed. Starting and operating a business involves uncertainty and risk. Outcomes vary based on factors including your experience, decisions, offer, implementation, consistency, market, audience, timing, resources, and circumstances outside anyone's control.

3. Case Studies, Metrics, and Examples

Any views, reach, revenue, engagement, client, or account-growth examples shown on this website or during the workshop are past results from specific circumstances. They are provided to illustrate experience and strategy—not to state what every attendee should expect.

Mekayla J Media has not established a generally expected financial or business result for workshop attendees. Your results may be materially different, including no financial result.

4. Practical Tools and Platforms

The workshop may show practical ways to use familiar tools such as email and Instagram. Mentioning a platform does not mean the platform sponsors, endorses, or is affiliated with CEO Mode or Mekayla J Media. Platform features, pricing, policies, and availability can change.

5. Your Implementation and Responsibility

The workshop is intended to support your thinking—not replace it. You are responsible for deepening your own understanding, adapting any strategy to your audience, checking your assumptions, and deciding what is appropriate for your business.

  • Research your market, customers, pricing, and competition.
  • Follow applicable business, advertising, tax, privacy, and licensing requirements.
  • Use your own judgment before spending money, making claims, or entering agreements.
  • Understand that implementation and consistency can improve your process but cannot guarantee an outcome.
